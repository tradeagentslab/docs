# Scam checklist

"AI trading" scams have multiplied. If you see any one of these ten signs, stop.

## Ten signs

1. **Guaranteed returns.** "Can't lose", "3% a day", "double your money". Real trading guarantees nothing.
2. **They want an API key that can trade or withdraw.** With that key, someone else trades your money.
3. **They want your money:** sent to them to "manage", or deposited on a platform or app they choose.
4. **You must pay to withdraw.** A "verification fee", "deposit", "unlock fee" or "tax". Paying doesn't get your money out.
5. **A "support agent", "mentor" or "assistant" messages you first.** Real projects don't.
6. **"AI picks" groups, paid signals, trade calls, copy trading.** Your money follows someone else's hand.
7. **Only screenshots of profits.** No full record you can check trade by trade.
8. **Fake apps and fake sites.** A domain one or two letters off the real one; an app you're asked to install from a chat link.
9. **Pressure to decide now.** "Last 3 spots", "closes tonight".
10. **Celebrity endorsements and fake news screenshots.** The celebrity never said it.

## Cases that happened

- Mainland China: "Baiyu Quant" in Wuhan promised 3–5% a day, blocked withdrawals in December 2025 citing a "risk review", then disappeared. ([Tencent News](https://news.qq.com/rain/a/20260727A09YQ400), [Sina Finance](https://finance.sina.cn/2026-03-14/detail-inhqyaqm3249902.d.html)) Since July 2026, nearly ten brokers, including Galaxy, Guosheng and Zhongtai, have warned about scams dressed up as AI stock tips and quant trading. ([Shanghai Securities News](https://paper.cnstock.com/html/2026-07/25/content_2248767.htm))
- Hong Kong: in May 2026 police warned about fake "AI crypto investment apps" after more than 70 cases and over HK$50 million in losses in one week. ([The Standard](https://www.thestandard.com.hk/news/article/333026/Police-warn-of-fake-AI-crypto-investment-apps-after-50m-losses-in-one-week))
- Taiwan: in 2024 a 73-year-old man in Kaohsiung put NT$820,000 of retirement savings into an "AI auto-trading" app. It showed NT$22.45 million; he was told to pay a 2% "verification fee" (about NT$450,000) before he could withdraw. ([Liberty Times](https://news.ltn.com.tw/news/society/breakingnews/4573529))
- United States: in May 2026 the SEC charged a Texas man over a $12.3 million scheme sold as an AI trading bot. ([Bloomberg Law](https://news.bloomberglaw.com/ip-law/texas-man-ran-12-3-million-ai-based-crypto-scheme-sec-says))
- United Kingdom: in April 2026 the FCA led 17 regulators in action against illegal finfluencers. Promoting financial products on social media without authorisation is illegal in the UK: one guilty plea so far, two more facing criminal proceedings. ([FCA](https://www.fca.org.uk/news/press-releases/fca-spearheads-global-action-stop-illegal-finfluencers))
- Exchanges: in October 2025 Binance banned more than 600 accounts for "unauthorized third-party tools", mostly on Binance Alpha. Under Binance's rules, that covers unapproved trading bots, API abuse and shared accounts. ([The Block](https://www.theblock.co/post/375278/binance-bans-more-than-600-accounts-over-unauthorized-third-party-tools))

## If it happens to you

- Stop: send no money, hand over no keys, install no app they send.
- Keep evidence: chats, transfer records, web addresses.
- Report it to your local police and your financial regulator.
- Contact your exchange only through its official website or app.
